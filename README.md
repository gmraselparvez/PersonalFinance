## What's new in 1.19

- **Rich item descriptions** — bold, italic, underline, lists, quotes and links.
- **Invoice preview** — press *Next* to see the finished invoice, then download, print or send it.
- **Safer sync** — your business details can never be wiped by an empty sync.
- Several receiving accounts, totals in any currency, and Mark as Paid adds to Income.

## Install

1. Download **`PersonalFinance-v1.19.dmg`** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Drag **PersonalFinance** into **Applications** and open it.

<details>
<summary><b>macOS says it can't verify the app?</b></summary>
<br>

Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** next to PersonalFinance, then confirm.

<img src="assets/gatekeeper/02-privacy-security-open-anyway.png" width="420" alt="Open Anyway">

Only do this for downloads from this repository's releases.
</details>
